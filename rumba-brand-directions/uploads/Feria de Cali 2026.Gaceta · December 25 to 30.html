§ 01 · Una invitación
Editor's note From Julian, in Cali

This is not a tour. It's a small, deliberate group of dancers.landing in Cali during the only week of the year the entire city becomes a dance floor.and being introduced to the scene the way locals know it.

You will not stand in lines. You will not eat dinner at a hotel buffet. You will not be in a club on a Tuesday because the bus brought you there. What you will do is dance with the right people, in the right rooms, on the right nights.and learn a little about why Cali sounds the way it does.

We handle hotels, transfers, classes, tickets, and the introductions. You bring shoes, an open week, and the willingness to say yes when someone asks you onto the floor.

Everything below is handled before you land. No hidden upsells.
